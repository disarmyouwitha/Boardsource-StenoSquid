# Source credits

Steno Squid is an independent community hardware accessory. The project references Boardsource Wireless Corne expansion documentation, Adafruit product 5634 and its example circuit, Winbond flash specifications, and nice!view dimensions from nice!keyboards. Source URLs and reviewed revisions are in references/sources.json.

Generic circuit symbols were authored for this project and earlier electrical studies. Standard KiCad footprints and 3D models retain their upstream license and library exception. See LICENSES/kicad-footprints-LICENSE.md. The original design has no selected redistribution license yet.

The Inkling silkscreen is a hand-drawn fan illustration of an Inkling in squid form from Nintendo’s Splatoon. [Nintendo character reference](https://splatoon.nintendo.com/en/world/)
