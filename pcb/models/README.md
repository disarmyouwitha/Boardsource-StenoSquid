# Flash assembly model

The WSON STEP model is adapted from KiCad's WDFN-8-1EP_6x5mm_P1.27mm_EP3.4x4mm model. Its exposed-pad face is extended from 3.4 × 4.0 mm to 3.4 × 4.3 mm to match the footprint. The body remains 6 × 5 × 0.8 mm. It is an assembly representation based on the package drawing.
